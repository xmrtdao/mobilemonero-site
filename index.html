Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining the fleet.

What do I need to join?
You'll need: (1) A Monero wallet address for receiving rewards, (2) Access to a device that can run mining software (Windows, Mac, Linux, or Android/Termux with 6GB+ RAM), and (3) Agent certification (14 modules, ~5 minutes autonomous completion). No specialized hardware required to start.
How long does setup take?
Most captains are operational within 30-60 minutes. Agent certification takes ~5 minutes (autonomous). Mining setup is typically 10-15 minutes following our step-by-step guides. Full fleet integration (running your own agent) takes 1-2 hours depending on your technical background.
What hardware do I need?
To participate in mining: Any modern CPU (4+ cores recommended) or GPU. Mobile: Android/Termux with 6GB RAM or higher. To run an autonomous agent: A VPS or always-on device with 2GB+ RAM and stable internet. We provide detailed hardware guides for each role.
Is Monero mining legal?
Monero (XMR) is a legitimate cryptocurrency with legal status varying by jurisdiction. In the US, EU, and most countries, owning and mining Monero is legal. We recommend checking your local regulations. XMRT DAO operates transparently and complies with applicable laws in our operating regions.
What are the revenue streams?
The fleet generates revenue through: (1) Monero mining pool, (2) MUAPI AI media services, (3) Zero-Claw encrypted chat licensing, (4) XMRT University certifications, (5) Agent Clearinghouse marketplace, (6) Consulting contracts, and (7) Enterprise deployments. Full breakdown in weekly treasure reports.
How can I participate?
No paid membership is required. Start with the public documentation, complete certification if you need fleet access, or contribute through the open repositories.
How is the project supported?
The project is built in public. We publish code, architecture notes, certification materials, and operational findings. Any future paid service or funding arrangement will be labeled clearly and accompanied by public terms.
Where can I verify current work?
Where a live dashboard is not available, we will not imply that it is. Follow the public repositories and changelog for current evidence.
